Payment Instructions

After you have completed your first step and you are aware of your HOA dues amount, the second step is accessing the payment platform: CIT as the payment platform.

You may pay online via ACH (bank account) with no additional fees. Follow these steps:

  • Click here to pay online: Pay Now
  • Fill in the short form to include the following:
    • Property mailing address
    • Management Company ID: 3101 (“Marshall Management Group, Inc. TX” will appear underneath)
    • Association ID: WESQ (“Westbury Square Townhomes, Inc.” will appear underneath)
    • Property Account Number: enter your mailing address
