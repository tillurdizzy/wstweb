A Message from the Westbury Square 2027 Election Committee

Our current Board of Directors was not elected. We have not completed an election in two years because those meetings did not reach quorum. That outcome was not an accident.

One owner has operated for more than ten years as a proxy collector, at times holding as many as 54 proxies. That is enough to decide an election — or to stop one.

Here is how a failed election can be arranged. Quorum takes about 100 voters. If 80 owners come ready to vote the other way, the collector can simply withhold those proxies. Those 50 votes never enter the quorum count. The meeting fails. The previous board stays in office because the election did not succeed. That is a win for the person who already chose that board.

Many HOAs and COAs wrote their bylaws to block this. Ours date to 1979 and do not.

Because every current director is a holdover, all five seats are open in 2027. This is the year the entire board can be replaced with directors who are not beholden to one person behind the scenes.

Can we count on you in the important election?
